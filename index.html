Let's Learn about Wolves!

In this section, we are learning the basics of wolf behavior.

Wolves are pack animals, and are very social creatures. They communicate with various sounds, gestures, and expressions.

Wolf Behaviors & Pack Dynamics:

  • Howling: Used to assemble the pack, locate missing members, and warn rival packs away.
  • Tail Position: High tail signals dominance and strength; a lowered tail signals submission.
  • Play Bowing: Lowering front legs while keeping the hind end up signals a desire to play.
  • Scent Marking: Marking territory boundaries to inform neighboring packs of their presence.
  • Hunting Strategies: Cooperative endurance hunting to tire out large prey over long distances.
